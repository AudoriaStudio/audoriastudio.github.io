Audoria privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Audoria is an audiobook player made by Audoria Studio, the trading name of its developer, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. Audoria Studio is responsible for any personal data covered by this policy.

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and Audoria Studio runs no server. The app does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your library (titles, authors, covers, chapters), listening positions, listening time (for your listening statistics), bookmarks, collections and settings are stored only on your phone. Audoria also writes a small save file (savedata.audoria.dat) into each book's folder so your progress survives a reinstall. Audoria only reads the folders and files you choose to add.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Audoria's library database and settings in your Google backup. That backup is made by Android, stored in your own Google account and encrypted by Google; Audoria Studio has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android's settings.

When Audoria connects to the internet

Audoria only goes online for the three things below. Books on your phone play without any connection.

Your Audiobookshelf server: if you add one, Audoria connects only to the addresses you enter, using the API key you provide, to list, stream and download your books and to keep your listening position in step. Your server addresses and key are stored only on your phone and are only ever sent to your own server.

Chromecast: when you cast, Audoria finds Cast devices on your Wi-Fi and sends the audio to the device you pick, using Google's Cast service on your phone. Books stored on your phone are streamed from your phone directly to that device over your local network.

Audoria Pro purchase: buying Audoria Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. Audoria never sees your payment details; it only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Audoria Pro. Google Play shows us a record of each order (for example the order number, date and price) so we can deal with refunds. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Permissions

Notifications: to show playback controls. Alarms: a backup for the sleep timer. Network: for the features above. Google Play billing: to check for the Audoria Pro purchase. Audoria has no access to your storage beyond the folders and files you pick, and no access to your contacts, location, microphone or phone calls (it notices calls only through Android's audio state, to pause).

Children

Audoria is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Audoria removes everything it stored on your phone. The save files in your book folders are yours to keep or delete. Audoria Studio holds nothing else about you, unless you email us (see below).

Emails you send us

If you email us, including through Contact us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you include, such as the app and phone details. We use them only to reply and to fix problems you report. We don't add you to any mailing list and don't share your email with anyone. We keep support emails only as long as we need them to help you, and delete them sooner if you ask. They are stored in our Gmail account, provided by Google.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it: email audoriastudio@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK, or to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app (Settings → About → Privacy policy) and on the store listing, with a new date.
