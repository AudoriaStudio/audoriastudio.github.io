Audoria testers

Thank you for helping test Audoria!

1. Join the test

  1. Make sure we have the Google account email you use for the Play Store on your Android phone. If you haven't sent it yet, email it to audoriastudio@gmail.com.
  2. On your phone, open the testing link we sent you and tap Become a tester (or Accept invite).
  3. Tap Download it on Google Play and install Audoria. It can take a few minutes to appear the first time.

Please keep Audoria installed for at least 14 days and open it every few days. Google only lets us release the app once enough testers have had it installed for 14 days in a row.

2. Try these

  • Tap Get Started, then choose a folder of audiobooks (MP3, M4B…). No audiobooks? Free public-domain ones are available from LibriVox.
  • Play a book: pause, skip, change speed, jump between chapters, add a bookmark.
  • Set a sleep timer and let it fade out.
  • Close the app and reopen it — does it remember your place?
  • Try the widget, your headphone or Bluetooth buttons, and Android Auto if your car has it.
  • All Pro features are free for your first 7 days: the equalizer, editing book details and covers, collections, player backgrounds.

3. Tell us what you think

Found a bug, a crash or something that looks wrong? Got an idea? Send as many reports as you like. Screenshots help — email them to audoriastudio@gmail.com.
